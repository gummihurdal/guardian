Why now

Three things changed in 2026. Backup management became a legal control, insurers started demanding tested restores, and a cyber-attack on supermarket freezers showed what an unpatched controller on the internet costs.

NIS2 names backups

Article 21(2)(c) requires business continuity "such as backup management and disaster recovery" as an evidenced control. Supply-chain clauses push the same questions down to small suppliers, even below the thresholds.

Insurers check restores

Cyber-insurance renewals now ask for isolated backup credentials, immutable copies and tested restores within the last 90 days, with documented results. No evidence, higher premium or no cover.

Freezers got hacked

In October 2026 attackers reached refrigeration controllers at hundreds of French supermarkets through software that could no longer be updated. Losses: tens of millions of euros in spoiled food. The vendor had already identified the risk.

"Attacking equipment that is essential to preserving fresh produce and the food supply of the French people is extremely serious and without precedent."Chief executive of Coopérative U, after the October 2026 attack, as reported by E24 and the BBC

How it works

Read-only connections and public data only. No agents on your production servers, no penetration testing, no exploitation, nothing changed without your say. You authorise exactly what we look at, in writing, before we look. Guardian observes and proves; it never attacks.

Connect

A read-only link to your backup system (Veeam, Proxmox Backup, Synology, Azure Backup) and a list of the public addresses and equipment you own.

Check

Every day we collect job results, retention, immutability and restore-test records, and scan your external footprint against known-vulnerable products: refrigeration, HVAC, cameras, UPS, door systems.

Report

Once a month you get a plain-language report with a traffic-light summary and a fix list in priority order, written for a managing director, not an engineer. Urgent findings come the same day.

Prove

Quarterly, a signed attestation of your restore tests and exposure status, formatted to answer insurer and NIS2 questionnaires directly.
