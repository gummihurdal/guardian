Verdict

Not nonsense. NIS2 Article 21(2)(c) makes backup management and disaster recovery an evidenced control. The Cyber Resilience Act's reporting duty has applied since 11 September 2026. Insurers' and large customers' questionnaires ask for recovery evidence. A founder who runs backups and servers for a living can build the collector cheaply.

But the external-exposure half is already free. Insurers give it away: Coalition Control is free even to non-policyholders, At-Bay includes scans with its policies, and Chubb gives policyholders BitSight and SecurityScorecard reports. A small company won't pay €199 a month for something its broker offers for nothing. Nobody cheaply checks and records that a restore was tested and worked, that an immutable copy exists, and that backup credentials are separate. That is the product.

New pitch: "Guardian proves, every quarter, that your backups actually restore, in a report your insurer and your biggest customer accept."
